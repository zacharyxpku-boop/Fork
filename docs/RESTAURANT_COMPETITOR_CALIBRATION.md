# Restaurant competitor calibration

This project should not describe competitor capability as "missing UI polish". The gap is mostly data access, account permission, and transaction/control surface.

## What public competitors already cover

- [Restaurant365](https://www.restaurant365.com/restaurant-back-office-software/) covers accounting, inventory, labor scheduling, payroll, reporting, and POS-linked back-office control.
- [Toast Loyalty](https://pos.toasttab.com/products/loyalty) sits inside a broader platform with POS, marketing, Guest CRM, online ordering, scheduling, inventory, benchmarking, and multilocation management.
- [OpenTable GuestCenter](https://www.opentable.com/restaurant-solutions/products/guestcenter/) combines reservations, restaurant marketing, guest profiles, reviews, reporting, and targeted marketing.

## What this means for Wenai

Wenai can already do the internal pieces that do not need external authority:

- turn a restaurant brief into a concrete action queue,
- generate channel-specific content drafts,
- keep evidence, owners, and next steps explicit,
- keep public POI samples bounded and attributed,
- show where a task stops because the platform is not connected.

Wenai cannot truthfully claim the following without outside setup:

- automatic publishing,
- automatic acquisition from platform traffic,
- automatic coupon redemption / verification,
- real operational analytics from POS, inventory, reservation, and review systems.

## Current product position

The right product claim is not "we already do what they do".
The right claim is:

1. we can convert a restaurant input into a runnable local operating plan,
2. we can keep the external boundary visible,
3. we can accept real external data once the merchant or platform permissions exist.
